How do I enter the weekly challenge?+

Download your meme or write an original idea, then post it in JOBBYChat with #JOBBYChallenge and your public display name before the deadline. Entries are reviewed manually for originality, humour and the current JOBBY identity. By entering, you allow JOBBY to feature your entry and public name on this site and official channels. There is no automatic upload, vote count or confirmed token reward.

Where does the project stand?+

JOBBY is a Solana meme project being built from the ground up. The current focus is the character, original content, and the community. Follow the official channels for confirmed launch updates.

Where can I find official updates?+

Follow @JOBBYSOL on X and the official Telegram channel. The community chat is JOBBYChat.

Does the meme lab post for me?+

No. You can download an image, copy a caption, or open an X draft. You choose whether and where to post it. The checklist records only what you mark on your device.
