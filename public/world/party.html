HOW TO PLAY

👑 Take the crown in Rune Hall to your home flag. Its carrier moves slowly. Stand near a teammate and tap PASS.

🧪 MINI fits the small tunnel. GIANT pushes the heavy crate. JELLY jumps higher. Refill at marked bottles.

🔀 Gates swap every minute with a six-second warning. They wait if a player is in the opening.

🎒 The cursed bag attracts the guardian. Bank it at the east treasure marker for 15 seconds of team protection.

💬 One player reads the west maze tablet; a different teammate enters the runes in the north hall. Use voice or signals. Solving opens all gates for 20 seconds.

🎬 Watch your round highlights after the finish.
