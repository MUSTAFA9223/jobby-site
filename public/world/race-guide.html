A little push.
A lot of chaos.

Race across a floating playground of wobbling platforms, spinning sweepers and trapdoors. Bring your friends, pick your hero, and turn every tumble into a comeback.

▶ SOLO PRACTICE👥 RACE WITH FRIENDS
01 / FOAM HOP

Find your feet.

Jump between soft purple platforms. Hop over the spinning sweeper instead of running straight into it. The mint platform saves your progress.

02 / SWAYING ISLANDS

Time the leap.

The islands slide from side to side and carry you with them. Wait for an opening, then jump. The yellow bumper can send you flying.

03 / TRAPDOOR TROUBLE

Keep moving.

Three lanes, three rhythms. A panel turns red before it drops. Change lanes or wait at a checkpoint — falling always brings you back.

04 / FINAL SPIN

Earn your finish.

Clear the last sweeper and cross the checkered stripe. Each racer gets their own finish time and place. The first finish keeps the race open for everyone else.

Small controls. Big comebacks.

  • Move: left joystick. Jump: big arrow on the right. You run automatically while moving.
  • Push: tap the small hand near jump. Aim at a nearby racer. It recharges every 2.2 seconds; checkpoint respawns have brief protection.
  • Camera: drag the open screen, pinch to zoom, or tap the crosshair to face down the course.
  • Desktop: WASD or arrow keys, Space to jump, E to push.
  • Points: +10 per checkpoint and +50 for finishing in solo practice. Private races save +80 for a verified full finish, once per player per round.
  • Play together: create a private room, share the code, and let the host start when 2–12 players are ready. Optional microphone and speaker buttons stay at the side.
Your current characters, profile and points still work. Warehouse Escape, Big House and Classic remain available. This is the first obstacle course; private race results receive server checkpoint and movement checks. Game points have no cash value.
